MAPO Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 28, 2026  ·  Last updated: March 28, 2026

MAPO ("we", "us", or "our") is a mobile application that helps musicians, venues, and fans discover and connect with each other. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you create an account or use MAPO:

  • Account information: your display name, username, and password (stored as a secure hash).
  • Profile content: bio, profile photo, and optional social links (Instagram, Bandcamp, website, email, phone) that you choose to add.
  • Usage data: shows you RSVP to, connections you make, and posts you create within the app.
  • Device proximity data: when you use the Nearby or NFC features, your device briefly scans for other MAPO users nearby via Bluetooth and the local network. No location coordinates are recorded or stored.

We do not collect your GPS location, browsing history, contacts, or any data unrelated to the MAPO experience.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your MAPO profile.
  • To display your profile to other users you connect with.
  • To show you upcoming shows and the people attending them.
  • To enable Nearby Discovery so you can connect with other MAPO users in the same room (opt-in only, stops when you leave the screen).
  • To improve the app and fix bugs (using anonymised analytics).

We do not sell your data. We do not use your data for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, a cloud database provider. Data is stored in the United States. Supabase employs industry-standard security measures including encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest.

4. Bluetooth and Local Network Access

The Nearby Discovery feature uses Bluetooth and the local Wi-Fi network to find other MAPO users in your immediate vicinity. This feature:

  • Only runs while you are on the Network screen and have not dismissed the feature.
  • Broadcasts only your MAPO username and node ID — never your GPS location.
  • Stops immediately when you leave the screen or close the app.
  • Does not store or transmit any Bluetooth or network scan data to our servers.

5. NFC Access

If you choose to use an NFC tag to share your profile, the app reads the tag to retrieve a MAPO user ID and look up that profile. No NFC scan data is stored beyond the connection record you choose to create.

6. Photo Library Access

MAPO requests access to your photo library only to let you set a profile picture. We do not scan, upload, or store any photos other than the one you explicitly select. Your photo is uploaded to our secure storage and linked to your profile.

7. Data Sharing

We share data only in the following limited circumstances:

  • With other MAPO users: your public profile (name, handle, bio, photo, social links) is visible to users you connect with.
  • Service providers: Supabase (database and storage). They are contractually prohibited from using your data for their own purposes.
  • Legal requirements: if required by law or to protect the safety of users.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. To delete your account and all associated data, email us at the address below. We will process deletion requests within 30 days.

9. Children's Privacy

MAPO is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by updating the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your data.
  • Object to or restrict our processing of your data.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address below.
